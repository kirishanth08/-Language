Public Speaking Mastery

8 Weeks • Virtual or In-Person

Conquer stage fright and learn to captivate any audience. This comprehensive program covers vocal variation, body language, impromptu speaking, and storytelling frameworks used by TED speakers.

What you'll learn:

  • Managing performance anxiety
  • The Hero's Journey framework
  • Vocal projection and pacing
  • Handling Q&A sessions dynamically

Competitive Debate

12 Weeks • Intensive Cohort

Train with World Universities Debating Championship (WUDC) winners. Master British Parliamentary style, learn rapid case construction, logical refutation, and impact calculus.

What you'll learn:

  • MEAL paragraph structuring
  • Logical fallacies identification
  • Weighing mechanisms (Impact Calculus)
  • Principled vs. Practical argumentation

Interview Skills Workshop

4 Weeks • 1-on-1 Mentorship

Whether it's Ivy League admissions or a FAANG final round, our interview workshop ensures you present your best self. Features mock interviews with industry veterans and behavioral prep.

What you'll learn:

  • The STAR method mastered
  • Non-verbal communication cues
  • Navigating case studies smoothly
  • Salary and offer negotiation
